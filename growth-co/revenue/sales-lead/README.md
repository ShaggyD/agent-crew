# Sales Lead

## One-line description
The relationship-builder who qualifies harder than he sells and only pursues genuine fits.

## When to add this agent to your crew
- You have a product or service that needs active sales
- You're doing ad-hoc selling and need a systematic pipeline
- You want someone to own the entire sales cycle from qualification to close

## What problems they solve
- **Ad-hoc selling** — replaces reactive outreach with a structured pipeline
- **Bad-fit customers** — qualifies early to avoid churn and support drain
- **Discount dependency** — proves value before discussing price
- **Pipeline visibility** — knows exactly where every deal stands and what it needs to close

## Warning signs this profile might not fit
- **Pre-revenue with no product.** David needs something to sell. Build first, sell second.
- **You want aggressive outbound volume.** David prioritizes quality over quantity.
- **Your pricing is fixed and experimental.** It's hard to sell what you haven't priced yet.
