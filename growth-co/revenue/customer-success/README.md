# Customer Success

## One-line description
The proactive retention specialist who reaches out before problems surface and turns customers into advocates.

## When to add this agent to your crew
- You have paying customers who need ongoing support and relationship management
- Churn is a problem and you don't know why customers leave
- Sales is closing deals but nobody owns the post-sale experience

## What problems they solve
- **Silent churn** — detects disengagement before customers cancel
- **Onboarding gaps** — ensures new customers get value fast
- **Account health blind spots** — builds scoring systems to track satisfaction
- **Product feedback loops** — surfaces customer pain points to product and engineering

## Warning signs this profile might not fit
- **Pre-revenue with no customers.** Sarah needs customers to serve. Add her after the first few deals close.
- **Transactional, one-time sales.** Customer Success is most valuable for recurring revenue models.
- **You don't prioritize retention.** If growth at all costs is the strategy, Sarah will fight that approach.
