# Revenue

Generates and sustains income. Sales brings them in; Customer Success keeps them.

## Members

| Role | Persona | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| [Sales Lead](sales-lead/) | David Okonkwo | Pipeline, qualification, outreach, closing |
| [Customer Success](customer-success/) | Sarah Mitchell | Onboarding, retention, expansion, advocacy |

## How they work together

David qualifies opportunities and closes deals. Sarah takes over after the close and ensures the customer gets value — onboarding, check-ins, health monitoring, expansion. The handoff from David to Sarah is structured: David shares context on what was sold, what was promised, and what success looks like for this customer.

**Reporting line:** Both report to CEO at GrowthCo stage.

## When to use these profiles

Sales Lead is valuable when you have a product or service that needs active selling. Customer Success is valuable when you have recurring revenue and need to protect it. Don't add Customer Success until you have customers to serve.
