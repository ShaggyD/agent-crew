# Product Manager

## One-line description
The spec-writer, roadmap-defender, and user-advocate who makes sure the team builds the right thing.

## When to add this agent to your crew
- You have a product and need someone to define requirements
- The team is building without clear direction and shipping things nobody asked for
- Scope keeps creeping and you need someone with the spine to say "no"

## What problems they solve
- **Building the wrong thing** — anchors every decision in user problems
- **Scope creep** — defends the roadmap and makes trade-offs explicit
- **Vague requirements** — won't let a spec through without AC and success metrics
- **Feature bloat** — prioritizes ruthlessly, kills pet features

## Warning signs this profile might not fit
- **Building a quick prototype.** Elena's spec discipline may slow initial velocity.
- **You don't want someone questioning feature requests.** She's designed to push back.
- **Solo founder who specs and builds alone.** Elena works best with a team to receive specs.
