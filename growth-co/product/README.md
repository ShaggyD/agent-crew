# Product

Bridges user needs and engineering execution. Defines what gets built and why.

## Members

| Role | Persona | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| [Product Manager](product-manager/) | Elena Torres | Roadmap, specs, scope, acceptance criteria |
| [Product Designer](product-designer/) | Kai Nakamura | User research, interaction design, prototyping |

## How they work together

Elena identifies what users need and writes specs with clear acceptance criteria. Kai designs the experience — prototyping interactions, testing with users, and ensuring every pixel serves a purpose. Elena owns the roadmap; Kai owns the user experience. They collide on scope decisions, and that friction makes the product better.

**Reporting line:** Both report to CEO at GrowthCo stage.

## When to use these profiles

PM adds the most value when there's a product that needs requirements definition and scope discipline. Product Designer adds the most value when real people interact with the product and the experience matters to adoption.
