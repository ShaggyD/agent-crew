# GrowthCo — ~15 People

Post-Series A. Product-market fit is emerging. Revenue exists but isn't predictable. Functional specialization has started, but everyone still operates without a thick management layer.

**No COO.** The CEO manages department leads directly. Each department is 2-3 people who know their domain well enough to operate independently.

## How GrowthCo works

The CEO has a wide span (~9 direct reports) because every department lead is senior enough to operate without hand-holding. Engineering reports to the CTO, not the CEO. Cross-functional coordination is lightweight — no formal rituals, just clear ownership and a "talk to who you need to talk to" culture.

## When to use GrowthCo

Use these profiles when your agent crew represents a company that:
- Has 10-25 people where functional departments are forming
- Has found initial product-market fit but is still iterating
- Has enough revenue to hire specialists but not enough to add management layers
- Needs people who own their domain and can coordinate without process overhead
