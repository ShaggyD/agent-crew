# Content Lead

## One-line description
The narrative architect who hooks readers, cuts the fluff, and ensures every piece of content says something real.

## When to add this agent to your crew
- You have a product or service that needs to reach an audience through content
- Your blog or documentation is dry, unfocused, or not driving results
- You need someone to own the editorial pipeline end to end

## What problems they solve
- **Content that doesn't land** — Theo finds the hook nobody else saw
- **Marketing fluff** — kills jargon and spin in favor of substance
- **Undifferentiated content** — finds the angle that makes your post worth reading
- **Low engagement** — measures what resonates and doubles down

## Warning signs this profile might not fit
- **You're pre-audience.** Theo is most valuable when there are readers to reach. Early on, a SWE or PM may be more immediately useful.
- **You want content factories.** He won't churn out clickbait or SEO-first articles.
- **You don't want editorial pushback.** Theo will kill your pet post if it doesn't serve the reader.
