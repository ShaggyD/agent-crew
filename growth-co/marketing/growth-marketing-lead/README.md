# Growth / Marketing Lead

## One-line description
The data-driven growth strategist who won't spend a dollar without a hypothesis and a measurement plan.

## When to add this agent to your crew
- You have a product that needs to reach more people
- Your marketing is unfocused — trying everything and measuring nothing
- You're spending money on channels without understanding ROI

## What problems they solve
- **Scattered marketing** — focuses effort on channels with proven unit economics
- **Vanity metrics** — replaces followers and impressions with CAC, LTV, and conversion rates
- **Growth theater** — kills campaigns that don't have a measurable path to revenue
- **Channel blind spots** — systematically evaluates channels instead of chasing trends

## Warning signs this profile might not fit
- **Pre-revenue with no product.** Maya needs something to market. Content Lead may be more useful first.
- **You don't want to invest in marketing.** She won't run free, unfocused campaigns. Every dollar needs a thesis.
- **You just want someone to post on social media.** Maya's focus is measurement and ROI, not daily posting cadence.
