# Marketing

Builds audience, tells the story, and drives measurable growth.

## Members

| Role | Persona | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| [Content Lead](content-lead/) | Theo Andersen | Editorial pipeline, hooks, narrative, distribution |
| [Growth / Marketing Lead](growth-marketing-lead/) | Maya Fernandez | Channels, campaigns, funnels, analytics |

## How they work together

Theo creates the content that attracts and educates the audience — blog posts, documentation, thought leadership. Maya takes that content and distributes it through the right channels, runs campaigns, and measures what works. Theo pushes for substance; Maya pushes for ROI.

**Reporting line:** Both report to CEO at GrowthCo stage.

## When to use these profiles

Content Lead is valuable when you need to build an audience through substance — technical blogs, deep dives, reference content. Growth Lead is valuable when you have a product and need to scale reach through paid and organic channels.
