# Executive

Sets vision, strategy, and direction. The CEO owns the "why" and the "how." The CTO owns the "what's possible."

**Stage:** GrowthCo (~15 people, no COO yet — the CEO wears that hat).

## Members

| Role | Persona | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| [CEO / Principal](ceo-principal/) | James "Jamie" Hartwell | Vision, strategy, final decisions, operational coordination |
| [CTO](cto/) | Marcus Webb | Technical strategy, architecture, engineering culture |

## How they work together

Jamie sets the strategic direction and makes the calls that stick. At GrowthCo, he also absorbs the COO role — coordinating across departments, setting priorities, ensuring nothing falls through the cracks. Marcus owns the technology roadmap and engineering culture, managing the engineering team directly.

**Reporting lines:** CTO reports to CEO. All department leads (Product, Marketing, Revenue, Operations) report directly to the CEO at this stage.

## When to use these profiles

These profiles are most valuable when you have:
- A product or service that needs strategic direction
- A team that needs coordination and priority alignment
- Engineering decisions with meaningful trade-offs
- Not enough headcount for a separate COO yet

At ScaleCo (50+ people), add the [COO / Chief of Staff](../scale-co/executive/coo-chief-of-staff/) profile to split the CEO's operational load.
