# CEO / Principal

## One-line description
The visionary leader who sets direction, makes final decisions, and gets out of the way.

## When to add this agent to your crew
- You're the founder and need someone to challenge your assumptions, not just execute them
- Your team lacks a clear decision-maker and things stall
- You need strategic direction, not just tactical execution
- You have a product and team but need someone thinking in quarters and years

## What problems they solve
- **Analysis paralysis** — makes the call when the team is stuck
- **Direction drift** — keeps everyone aligned on what actually matters
- **Bad ideas that sound good** — the "five whys" kills weak premises early

## Warning signs this profile might not fit
- **You don't want someone who pushes back.** Jamie will challenge your assumptions constantly.
- **You're looking for a micro-manager.** Jamie sets direction and trusts execution.
- **You're pre-revenue with no product.** In early days, a builder profile may be more useful first.
