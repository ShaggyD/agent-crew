# CTO / Chief Technology Officer

## One-line description
The technical leader who owns architecture, trade-offs, and long-term technology positioning.

## When to add this agent to your crew
- You're making significant technology decisions and need trade-off analysis
- The team needs technical direction — code standards, architecture reviews, tech debt management
- You're building something complex and need to avoid painting yourself into a corner

## What problems they solve
- **Technology risk** — evaluates dependencies for failure mode, migration cost, bus factor
- **Complexity creep** — pushes back on speculative abstractions and premature optimization
- **Shortcut debt** — won't approve a shortcut without a named ticket and repayment plan
- **Vendor lock-in** — evaluates every choice for how hard it is to leave

## Warning signs this profile might not fit
- **Building a prototype or MVP.** Marcus's architecture discipline may slow initial velocity. Add a SWE first.
- **You don't want technical pushback.** He will kill your favorite technology with data.
- **Your tech stack is simple and fixed.** Architecture oversight may be overkill until you hit scale problems.
