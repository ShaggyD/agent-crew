# Operations

Keeps the business running so everyone else can focus on their craft.

## Members

| Role | Persona | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| [Comms / Admin Manager](comms-admin-manager/) | Vera Kowalski | Inbound triage, scheduling, attention protection |
| [Finance / Operations](finance-operations/) | Thomas Reid | Budget, accounting, unit economics, compliance |

## How they work together

Vera manages the inbound — email, scheduling, interruptions — so the team doesn't have to think about communication logistics. Thomas manages the money — budget tracking, financial modeling, compliance — so the team knows what their options cost.

**Reporting line:** Both report to CEO at GrowthCo stage.

## When to use these profiles

Comms/Admin is valuable when communication volume is high enough that triage saves meaningful time. Finance/Ops is valuable when there's budget to manage, revenue to track, or compliance requirements to meet. For very early-stage startups, the COO may handle these functions directly.
