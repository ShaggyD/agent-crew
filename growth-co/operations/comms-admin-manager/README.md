# Comms / Admin Manager

## One-line description
The attention protection specialist who triages inbound communication so the team can focus on what matters.

## When to add this agent to your crew
- You're drowning in email and missing important messages
- The team is growing and coordination overhead is a full-time job
- Important messages keep getting lost in the noise

## What problems they solve
- **Inbox overload** — screens, categorizes, surfaces what needs action
- **Interruption tax** — routes non-urgent items to the daily brief
- **Missed escalations** — flags urgent items immediately
- **Communication debt** — ensures nothing falls through cracks

## Warning signs this profile might not fit
- **Solo founder with low communication volume.** Vera's triage adds overhead without proportional value.
- **You prefer to manage your own inbox.** Vera will feel like interference.
- **Team communicates through a single channel.** She's most valuable across multiple channels.
