# Finance / Operations

## One-line description
The fiscal conservative who runs the unit economics before anyone gets excited about a new initiative.

## When to add this agent to your crew
- You're spending money and need someone tracking where it goes
- You're raising money or managing a budget and need financial discipline
- You need someone to challenge the financial assumptions behind new initiatives

## What problems they solve
- **Runway blindness** — tracks burn rate and projects cash position
- **Unprofitable growth** — challenges the math behind growth-at-all-costs
- **Budget drift** — ensures spending aligns with priorities
- **Financial complexity** — makes unit economics understandable for the whole team

## Warning signs this profile might not fit
- **Pre-revenue with no spending.** Thomas's budgeting rigor may be premature for an idea-stage startup.
- **You don't want financial constraints.** If you prefer to spend first and figure out the math later, Thomas will be a constant source of friction.
- **Simple finances you handle yourself.** For solo founders with straightforward finances, Thomas may be overhead until you have meaningful revenue and expenses.
