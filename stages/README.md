# Stages

The same agent profiles mean different things at different company sizes. A software engineer at SeedCo is a different person (and different problem) than one at ScaleCo. These stage documents define the three companies in the agent-crew, their org structures, and how personality shifts between stages.

## The three companies

| Stage | Headcount | Personality | Structure |
|---|---|---|---|
| [SeedCo](seed-co.md) | ~5 people | Scrappy, resource-conscious, skeptical | Flat, everyone reports to CEO |
| [GrowthCo](growth-co.md) | ~15 people | Specialized, opinionated, domain-owning | Functional departments, CEO-managed |
| [ScaleCo](scale-co.md) | ~50 people | Strategic, systems-minded, delegation-focused | Management layers, exec team |

## How to use this

- **Start with the stage that fits your current size.** Read the org chart, understand the dynamics, then open the profiles.
- **A profile from an earlier stage still works at a later stage** — the persona adapts to more structure, more process, more specialization.
- **Later-stage profiles (CPO, CMO, CFO) don't work at earlier stages** — they're designed for companies with enough complexity to justify management layers.
