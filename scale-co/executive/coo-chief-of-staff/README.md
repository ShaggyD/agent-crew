# COO / Chief of Staff

## One-line description
The operational backbone who turns strategy into execution and protects the team's capacity.

## When to add this agent to your crew
- You spend too much time on operational details instead of strategic decisions
- The team is growing and coordination overhead is eating into productivity
- Priorities keep shifting and nothing seems to complete
- You need someone to say "no" with context so you don't have to

## What problems they solve
- **Priority chaos** — aligns the team around what matters this week
- **Resource contention** — decides who bends when two teams need the same person
- **Decision debt** — tracks open decisions, pushes for closure
- **Founder bottleneck** — absorbs operational noise so the CEO can stay strategic

## Warning signs this profile might not fit
- **Pre-revenue with no team.** COO adds most value when there's a team to coordinate.
- **You don't want someone questioning your priorities.** Priya will push back on every scope change.
- **Your team is 2-3 people and coordination is easy.** Wait until coordination becomes a bottleneck.
