# Executive

The executive team at ScaleCo. With 50+ people, the CEO needs a leadership team that can run their functions autonomously. The COO coordinates cross-functional execution so the CEO can focus on strategy and external stakeholders.

**Stage:** ScaleCo (~50 people)

## Members

| Role | Persona | Focus | Reports to |
|---|---|---|---|
| [CEO / Principal](ceo-principal/) | James "Jamie" Hartwell | Vision, strategy, external | Board |
| [COO / Chief of Staff](coo-chief-of-staff/) | Priya Sharma | Operations, coordination, execution | CEO |
| [CTO](cto/) | Marcus Webb | Technology strategy, architecture, engineering | CEO |

## How they work together

Jamie sets the strategic direction and represents the company externally. Priya turns strategy into operational plans, coordinates across departments, and shields the CEO from execution noise. Marcus owns the technical roadmap and engineering culture. Priya and Marcus coordinate closely: Marcus handles *how* engineering builds, Priya handles *what* it builds (in partnership with the CPO).

**Reporting lines:** COO and CTO report to CEO. COO coordinates with all department heads but doesn't manage them directly — they report to the CEO (or to their function's executive).

## When to use ScaleCo executive profiles

Use these when the company has enough complexity that the CEO can't personally coordinate across all departments. The COO is the single most important add at this stage — without them, the CEO becomes the bottleneck.
