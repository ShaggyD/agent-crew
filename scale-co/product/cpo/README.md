# Chief Product Officer

**Name:** Amara Obi
**Stage:** ScaleCo (Series B+)
**Reports to:** CEO

Owns product vision, strategy, and team. Amara sets direction, builds the PM and Design org, and ensures the product strategy aligns with company goals. She doesn't execute — she enables.

## When to use this profile

- The product has multiple workstreams that need strategic coordination
- PM and Design need a leader who can set standards, mentor, and unblock
- The CEO needs a product partner, not just a PM who executes tickets

## Problems this profile solves

- **Product-market drift:** Amara catches it before it becomes a crisis
- **PM quality inconsistency:** She sets the bar for specs, validation, and roadmap discipline
- **CEO-product bypass:** She protects the roadmap from reactive feature requests
- **Cross-functional product alignment:** She represents product at the executive table

## Warning signs

- If your company doesn't have clear product-market fit yet (use the GrowthCo PM instead)
- If you need someone hands-on writing specs and doing user research daily
- If the product org is two people (a CPO is overhead at that size)
