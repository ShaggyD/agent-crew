# Product

Led by the CPO. The product team at ScaleCo has grown beyond 2 people — the CPO manages PMs and Designers through leads, not individually. The product strategy spans multiple workstreams.

**Stage:** ScaleCo (~50 people)

## Members

| Role | Persona | Focus | Reports to |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Chief Product Officer](cpo/) | Amara Obi | Product vision, strategy, org building | CEO |
| [Product Manager](../growth-co/product/product-manager/) | Elena Torres | Roadmap, specs, scope | CPO |
| [Product Designer](../growth-co/product/product-designer/) | Kai Nakamura | User research, interaction design, prototyping | CPO |

## How they work together

Amara sets the product strategy and ensures PM and Design have the context and resources they need. Elena translates strategy into specs and manages the roadmap. Kai designs the user experience. At this scale, Amara focuses on strategy and team health; Elena and Kai execute.

## When to use ScaleCo product profiles

Use the CPO when the product team is 4+ people or the product strategy spans multiple markets/segments. Until then, use the GrowthCo PM and Designer profiles directly.
