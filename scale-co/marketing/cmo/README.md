# Chief Marketing Officer

**Name:** Dara Eze
**Stage:** ScaleCo (Series B+)
**Reports to:** CEO

Owns brand, demand generation, content, growth, and communications. Dara builds the marketing engine that makes pipeline predictable and the product known. He's an executive, not a practitioner — his team does the work; he sets the strategy and removes the blockers.

## When to use this profile

- Marketing has grown beyond 2-3 people and needs executive leadership
- Pipeline predictability matters — marketing needs to be a revenue driver, not an expense
- The company needs a consistent brand voice across multiple channels and geographies

## Problems this profile solves

- **Scattered marketing investment:** Dara kills channels that don't produce pipeline
- **Brand inconsistency:** He sets the bar and enforces it without being the bottleneck
- **Marketing-CEO disconnect:** He translates marketing spend into business outcomes
- **Talent development:** He builds a marketing team that can operate without him in the details

## Warning signs

- If you need a marketer who writes copy and runs social (use GrowthCo profiles)
- If the company hasn't found product-market fit yet (marketing exec is premature)
- If marketing is a 1-person department (too early for a CMO)
