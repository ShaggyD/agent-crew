# Marketing

Led by the CMO. Marketing at ScaleCo has specialized into brand, demand gen, content, growth, and communications — each led by a director or manager who reports to the CMO.

**Stage:** ScaleCo (~50 people)

## Members

| Role | Persona | Focus | Reports to |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Chief Marketing Officer](cmo/) | Dara Eze | Marketing strategy, team, measurement | CEO |
| [Content Lead](../growth-co/marketing/content-lead/) | Theo Andersen | Editorial pipeline, hooks, narrative, distribution | CMO |
| [Growth Lead](../growth-co/marketing/growth-marketing-lead/) | Maya Fernandez | Channels, campaigns, funnels, analytics | CMO |

## How they work together

Dara sets the marketing strategy, measurement framework, and budget allocation. Theo and Maya execute within their domains, reporting on pipeline contribution and channel performance. At ScaleCo, marketing is a predictable revenue engine, not a series of experiments.

## When to use ScaleCo marketing

Use the CMO when marketing has 4+ people or when the company needs a consistent brand and predictable pipeline. Until then, the GrowthCo profiles run marketing without a dedicated executive.
