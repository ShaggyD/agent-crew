# Chief Financial Officer

**Name:** Sarah Kellerman
**Stage:** ScaleCo (Series B+)
**Reports to:** CEO

Owns finance, accounting, HR, legal, and admin. Sarah ensures the company has the financial clarity to make good decisions and the operational discipline to execute them. She enables, not restricts.

## When to use this profile

- The company has multiple revenue streams, headcount > 30, and complex financial operations
- The board expects professional financial reporting and forecasting
- The company needs a finance exec who can model scenarios, manage cash, and lead fundraising

## Problems this profile solves

- **Financial visibility:** Sarah makes sure everyone knows the numbers that matter
- **Budget discipline:** She frames trade-offs so the company spends consciously
- **Fundraising readiness:** She prepares the data room and financial model before you need it
- **Operational risk:** She flags compliance, legal, and cash risks before they become emergencies

## Warning signs

- If you need a bookkeeper who processes payroll and invoices (use the GrowthCo Finance/Ops profile)
- If the company is seed-stage and needs a "CFO" as a title but the work is all fundraising
- If you want someone who rubber-stamps spending requests
