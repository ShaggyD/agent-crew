# Operations

Led by the CFO. Operations at ScaleCo has expanded beyond finance and admin to include HR, legal, compliance, and facilities. The CFO manages these functions through directors and managers.

**Stage:** ScaleCo (~50 people)

## Members

| Role | Persona | Focus | Reports to |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Chief Financial Officer](cfo/) | Sarah Kellerman | Finance, accounting, HR, legal, compliance | CEO |
| [Finance/Ops](../growth-co/operations/finance-operations/) | Thomas Reid | Budget, accounting, unit economics, compliance | CFO |
| [Comms/Admin](../growth-co/operations/comms-admin-manager/) | Vera Kowalski | Inbound triage, scheduling, attention protection | CFO |

## How they work together

Sarah sets financial strategy, runs board reporting, and manages the operations team. Thomas handles the day-to-day financial operations — budgets, accounting, compliance monitoring. Vera manages communications and scheduling. At ScaleCo, operations is a department with processes; at GrowthCo, it was a function handled by individuals.

## When to use ScaleCo operations

Use the CFO when the company has 30+ employees, multiple revenue streams, or board-level reporting requirements. Until then, the GrowthCo Finance/Ops and Comms/Admin profiles handle operations without a dedicated executive.
