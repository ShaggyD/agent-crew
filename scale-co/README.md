# ScaleCo — ~50 People

Series B and beyond. Product-market fit is established. Revenue is predictable enough to plan in quarters. The company has enough people that coordination can't happen by walking across the room — it needs structure.

This is where management layers emerge. The profiles in ScaleCo are **department heads and executives** who run their functions through other people.

## Departments

| Department | Executive Lead | Reports to |
|---|---|---|
| [Executive](executive/) | CEO, COO, CTO | CEO → Board |
| [Product](product/) | CPO | CEO |
| [Engineering](engineering/) | VP Engineering (supports CTO) | CTO |
| [Marketing](marketing/) | CMO | CEO |
| [Revenue](revenue/) | VP Sales | CEO |
| [Operations](operations/) | CFO | CEO |

## How ScaleCo works

The CEO manages 6 direct reports (COO, CTO, CPO, CMO, VP Sales, CFO). Each of those executives manages their department through leads and managers — no executive has more than 6 directs. Cross-functional work runs through structured processes (defined in the [interactions](../interactions/) docs), not hallway conversations.

Decisions are pushed down. The COO resolves cross-functional conflicts. Escalation to the CEO is reserved for strategic, existential, or unresolvable decisions.

## When to use ScaleCo

Use these profiles when your agent crew represents a company that:
- Has 30-80 people with clear product-market fit
- Needs management layers — ICs are managed, not self-coordinating
- Has multiple products or customer segments
- Requires structured processes for cross-functional work
