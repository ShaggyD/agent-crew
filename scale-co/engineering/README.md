# Engineering

Led by the CTO with the VP Engineering managing day-to-day execution. The engineering team at ScaleCo has grown large enough to need management layers — leads, seniors, and IC tracks.

**Stage:** ScaleCo (~50 people)

## Members

| Role | Persona | Focus | Reports to |
|---|---|---|---|
| [VP Engineering](vp-engineering/) | Dana Reeves | Engineering execution, team health, delivery | CTO |
| [Software Engineer](../growth-co/engineering/software-engineer/) | Jordan Kwon | Implementation, readable code, code review | VP Eng |
| [QA Engineer](../growth-co/engineering/qa-engineer/) | Samira Patel | Test automation, regression suites, release gating | VP Eng |
| [DevOps Engineer](../growth-co/engineering/devops-engineer/) | Alex Chen | CI/CD, infrastructure, monitoring | VP Eng |

## How they work together

The CTO sets technical direction and architecture. Dana manages the engineering team day-to-day — sprint planning, 1:1s, hiring, delivery tracking. Jordan, Samira, and Alex operate within a structured cadence with defined processes for code review, QA gating, and deployment. At ScaleCo, engineering follows the full SDLC pipeline defined in [interactions](../../interactions/sdlc-pipeline.md).

## When to use ScaleCo engineering

Add the VP Engineering when the team is 8+ engineers. Until then, the CTO manages the team directly using the GrowthCo structure.
