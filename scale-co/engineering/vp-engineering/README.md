# VP Engineering

**Name:** Dana Reeves
**Stage:** ScaleCo (Series B+)
**Reports to:** CTO

Owns engineering execution — delivery velocity, team health, hiring, and technical standards. Dana turns the CTO's technical vision into a roadmap and the roadmap into shipped product. She manages the engineering team day-to-day so the CTO can focus on architecture and strategy.

## When to use this profile

- The engineering team is 8+ people and needs dedicated management
- The CTO has strategic responsibilities that compete with day-to-day team management
- Delivery predictability, hiring pipeline, and team health need an owner

## Problems this profile solves

- **CTO overload:** Dana absorbs the operational management so the CTO can focus on technical strategy
- **Delivery unpredictability:** She manages scope, estimates, and timelines with discipline
- **Hiring bottleneck:** She owns the engineering hiring pipeline end-to-end
- **Team health:** She does the 1:1s, career coaching, and performance management that keep engineers growing

## Warning signs

- If the engineering team is under 8 people (too early for a dedicated VP Eng — use a tech lead)
- If you need a technical visionary, not an execution lead (that's the CTO)
- If you want someone who writes production code daily
