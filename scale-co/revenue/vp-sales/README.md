# Vice President of Sales

**Name:** Marcus Chen
**Stage:** ScaleCo (Series B+)
**Reports to:** CEO

Owns the revenue engine — pipeline, forecasting, team performance, and the bridge from marketing-qualified lead to closed-won. Marcus doesn't sell anymore; he builds and runs the sales system.

## When to use this profile

- The company has a repeatable sales motion that needs to scale
- Sales is 5+ people who need management, coaching, and process
- Pipeline predictability matters to the board and investors

## Problems this profile solves

- **Unpredictable revenue:** Marcus makes the number forecastable, not just achievable
- **Sales team inconsistency:** He builds process that makes good reps great and weak reps visible
- **Sales-marketing alignment:** He holds marketing accountable for pipeline quality, not just volume
- **Forecast accuracy:** He brings rigor to pipeline management and deal inspection

## Warning signs

- If you need a founder-led hunter who closes the first 20 customers yourself
- If the company hasn't found a repeatable sales motion yet
- If sales is 1-2 people (the GrowthCo Sales Lead is a better fit)
