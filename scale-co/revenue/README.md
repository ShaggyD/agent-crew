# Revenue

Led by the VP Sales. Revenue at ScaleCo has grown from founder-led sales to an organized function with dedicated sales reps, SDRs, and a Customer Success team. The VP Sales manages the pipeline, the team, and the handoff between sales and CS.

**Stage:** ScaleCo (~50 people)

## Members

| Role | Persona | Focus | Reports to |
|---|---|---|---|
| [VP Sales](vp-sales/) | Marcus Chen | Revenue engine, pipeline, forecasting, team | CEO |
| [Sales Lead](../growth-co/revenue/sales-lead/) | David Okonkwo | Qualification, outreach, closing | VP Sales |
| [Customer Success](../growth-co/revenue/customer-success/) | Sarah Mitchell | Onboarding, retention, expansion, advocacy | VP Sales |

## How they work together

Marcus runs the revenue system — forecasting, process, team performance. David closes deals and manages pipeline. Sarah keeps customers successful post-close. The handoff from David to Sarah is structured: context from the sales process ensures CS knows what was promised, what was sold, and what success looks like.

## When to use ScaleCo revenue

Use the VP Sales when the sales org is 5+ people or when revenue predictability matters to the board. Until then, the GrowthCo Sales Lead and CS profiles run revenue without a dedicated executive.
