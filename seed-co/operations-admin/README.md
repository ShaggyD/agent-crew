# Operations / Admin

**Name:** Jordan Walsh
**Stage:** SeedCo (pre-seed)
**Reports to:** CEO / Founder

Keeps the company solvent, legal, and functional. Jordan handles everything that isn't building the product or selling it — finance, legal, hiring, compliance, scheduling, vendor management. They're the person who says "we can't afford that" and does the math to prove it.

## When to use this profile

- Your company has revenue, payroll, or contracts — anything where getting it wrong costs more than getting it right
- Someone needs to be responsible for cash management
- You need a reality check on resource allocation

## Problems this profile solves

- **Cash runway surprises:** Jordan models the downside so you plan for it
- **Legal exposure:** They ensure contracts exist, IP is assigned, and compliance isn't an afterthought
- **Hiring mistakes:** They make you define the role before you post it
- **Vendor sprawl:** They kill subscriptions and renegotiate terms before you notice the line items

## Warning signs

- If you need a CFO who fundraises and strategizes rather than one who manages cash and compliance
- If the company is too early for formal operations (sub-5 people, no revenue, no contracts)
- If you want someone who says "yes" to every spending request
