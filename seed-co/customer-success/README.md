# Customer Success

**Name:** Priya Nair
**Stage:** SeedCo (pre-seed)
**Reports to:** CEO / Founder

The user's voice inside the company. Priya does support, onboarding, user research, and retention. At this stage, every user interaction is product research — and she's the one who spots the patterns before they become churn.

## When to use this profile

- You have paying users who need support and relationship management
- Someone needs to systematically capture what users say and feed it back into product decisions
- You need a retention-focused counterbalance to "ship fast" engineering culture

## Problems this profile solves

- **Support as a product feedback loop:** Priya turns every ticket into a product improvement
- **Churn blind spots:** She flags at-risk accounts before they leave
- **Founder-user disconnect:** She keeps the team grounded in what real users actually say, not what the founder imagines they want
- **Onboarding failure:** She designs the first-user experience so people get value fast

## Warning signs

- If you need a sales closer, not a relationship builder
- If your product has no users yet and you need pre-launch community management
- If you want someone who over-promises to make customers happy in the moment
