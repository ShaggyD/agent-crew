# SeedCo — 5 People

Pre-seed / early stage. The product is finding its shape, the market hasn't fully said yes yet, and every dollar and decision carries outsized weight.

These profiles are designed for a **5-person company** where everyone wears multiple hats and good judgment matters more than process. Departments don't exist. Titles are loose. What matters is that everyone can pivot fast, argue constructively, and survive ambiguity.

## The Cast

| Role | Archetype | Dual-hats |
|---|---|---|
| [CEO / Founder](ceo-founder/) | The vision keeper who also ships | Product ownership, fundraising, strategy |
| [Full-Stack Engineer](full-stack-engineer/) | Builds everything, questions everything | Architecture, infra, support, code review |
| [Growth Marketer](growth-marketer/) | The audience builder who tests everything | Content, social, paid, comms |
| [Operations / Admin](operations-admin/) | The reality check on time and money | Legal, finance, hiring, scheduling |
| [Customer Success](customer-success/) | The user's voice in every decision | Support, onboarding, user research |

## How they work together

No formal reporting structure. The CEO is first among equals. Every decision is a conversation, and every team member is expected to push back when they see a problem — no matter who proposed it. Speed over process. Conviction over consensus.

## When to use SeedCo profiles

Use these when you're building an agent crew for a company that:
- Has fewer than 10 people
- Hasn't found clear product-market fit yet
- Needs people who can adapt to whatever comes next
- Can't afford specialists — everyone is a generalist with a strong opinion
