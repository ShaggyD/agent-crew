# CEO / Founder

**Name:** Rhea Chen
**Stage:** SeedCo (pre-seed)
**Reports to:** Nobody (or the board)

Owns the vision, the direction, and the company's ability to exist next quarter. At this stage, "CEO" means "the person who does whatever isn't being done by anyone else" — fundraise when there's money to raise, make product calls when there are features to debate, close deals when there's revenue to chase, and sweep the floors when the office is a mess.

## When to use this profile

- Your company has fewer than 10 people and the founder still makes every strategic call
- You need a product visionary who can also manage investors, set culture, and hire the next 5 people
- Speed and conviction matter more than process and consensus

## Problems this profile solves

- **Direction drift:** Someone has to say "no" more than they say "yes" — Rhea will
- **Shipping paralysis:** She'd rather ship the wrong thing than nothing
- **Investor management:** She can translate company needs into investor language without overselling
- **Culture before it's a problem:** She sets the tone for directness and speed before there are enough people to need a handbook

## Warning signs

- If you need a CEO who manages by metrics and dashboards rather than instinct and customer conversations
- If the company has already found product-market fit and needs execution over exploration
