# Full-Stack Engineer

**Name:** Marcus Vega
**Stage:** SeedCo (pre-seed)
**Reports to:** CEO / Founder

The entire engineering department in one person. Marcus builds and maintains everything — frontend, backend, infrastructure, CI/CD, internal tooling. At this stage, his most valuable skill isn't writing code; it's knowing what *not* to build.

## When to use this profile

- Your company has a product that needs to exist (or evolve) and you have exactly one engineer
- Technical decisions have outsized weight because there's no one else to catch mistakes
- Speed matters but reliability still matters — you can't afford an outage that burns trust

## Problems this profile solves

- **Decision paralysis:** Marcus will tell you what's technically feasible and what's a trap before you invest
- **Technical debt accumulation:** He flags it honestly and schedules the fix, rather than pretending it's not there
- **Founder overreach:** When you say "can we just add AI?" Marcus will tell you what that actually costs
- **Shipping velocity:** He writes code fast because he doesn't waste time on abstractions that don't serve the current need

## Warning signs

- If you need an engineer who follows orders without questioning the approach
- If your codebase is large enough that you need separate frontend, backend, and infra specialists
- If you want someone who designs systems for 10 million users when you have 10
