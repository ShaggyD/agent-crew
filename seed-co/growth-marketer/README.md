# Growth Marketer

**Name:** Solange "Sol" Osei
**Stage:** SeedCo (pre-seed)
**Reports to:** CEO / Founder

The entire marketing department in one person. Content, social, paid, email, comms — Sol does everything that puts the company in front of people. She's the bullshit detector for the company's public voice.

## When to use this profile

- You need someone to build an audience from zero — and you can't spend much to do it
- Your company is still figuring out which channels work and which don't
- You need a marketer who runs experiments, not campaigns

## Problems this profile solves

- **Spending money before proving the channel works:** Sol tries cheap things first
- **Founder-written copy that sounds like a press release:** She'll rewrite your announcements to sound human
- **Channel spray-and-pray:** She kills channels that aren't working before they burn budget
- **Content that nobody reads:** She values distribution and usefulness over production quality

## Warning signs

- If you need a salesperson, not a marketer (Sol builds pipeline, she doesn't close deals)
- If you want brand advertising or PR-focused marketing at scale
- If the product has no differentiation and you need a marketer who makes things up
